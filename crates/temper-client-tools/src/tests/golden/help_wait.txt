wait SUBJECT [--since DIGEST] [--until CONDITION] [--timeout DURATION]
Wait for a watch's change or condition
Result: wait
  subject: Watch subject
  since: Wait for a different digest
  until: Wait for a condition
  timeout: Timeout, 300 seconds by default
Subjects and --until conditions:
  me: waiting
  inbox: waiting|updates (no subject flags)
  task N: ended|waiting-on-me|held|replied|phase:<phase>
  tree N: ended
  run N: ended
  tasks: no condition; its read arguments except --after and --ended
Live phases: waiting|idle|due|preparing|claimed|running|backing-off|active|closing|held
Use --until ended for done, failed, cancelled or ended; terminal phase conditions are usage errors.
waiting-on-me covers the task's tree. An ended task answers with its ended read.
--since is the matching read's digest of the watched document, not its cursor.
--since and --until together require both change and condition.
Timeout: 300 seconds by default, at most 3600 seconds.
