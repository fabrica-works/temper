# temper-13.s3 A wake that overtakes a fresh-read chain is kept

`reports/temper-13-20261020T131332Z.md`, trace
`logs/20261020T131105-3841.log`). 13.5 rebases onto it and finishes.

Scope: `domain.rs`'s `step_change` returns, with no trace, when a step
for the same task is pending ("A timer or a delegate result can wake this
procedure while its prior fresh-read chain is still in flight"). When
OpenPull's outcome or hint wakes the change while a chain that read the
pull Missing before it is in flight, that wake is lost, and the chain
then decides on `pull = None`: `Made`, and a wait. domain/forge.md 5.3:
"Before a decision depends on a fact, the change child domain asks for
it afresh". So a wake during a chain is kept, and once the chain
finishes, the procedure steps again from a fresh read made after it.
Needs: nothing; it starts at once, before 13.5 goes on.
Budget: five runs and 30 minutes past the first failing test; past it,
raise it with each run's finding.
Files: `crates/temper-engine-domain-forge/src/domain.rs` (`step_change`,
`finish_step`, and the pending step's record), its `src/tests.rs`; and,
only if the decision's side needs it,
`crates/temper-engine-domain/src/route/forge_route.rs` (`change_decision`).
Shape: the pending step marks that it was woken again; `finish_step`
saves its row as now, then, when marked, starts the next step from
Branch, as `step_change` would. One wake
kept, however many came: a bool, not a queue. No new limit.

has read its pull Missing, woken by a Made OpenPull's outcome before the
chain ends, steps again and reads the pull afresh after the chain,

single step. Fixtures from `src/tests/fixtures.rs`.
